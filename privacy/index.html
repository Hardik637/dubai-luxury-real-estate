Legal Governance & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

At Vision of Excellence (Vision Excellence Properties), we are committed to respecting your privacy and protecting your personal information, whether you are seeking to buy a property, sell a property, or merely browsing our website. We recognize our obligation to keep sensitive information secure, and this Privacy Policy outlines the information management practices applied on this website to give you complete peace of mind.

By using this site, you signify your acceptance of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree to this Privacy Policy, please do not use the website. Vision Excellence Properties reserves the right to modify this Policy at any time by posting updates on this page. Your continued use of the Site following the posting of changes indicates your acceptance of those changes.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily submit to us, for example when you:

  • Submit an inquiry regarding a specific property, residential development, or investment scenario;
  • Request detailed brochures, master plans, floor plans, or structured payment schedules;
  • Submit a general contact or call-back request through our online consultation forms;
  • Contact our advisory team directly via telephone, WhatsApp, email, or digital messaging channels.

The personal information collected may include your full name, email address, telephone / WhatsApp number, residency status, budget parameters, and preferences regarding property searches.

2. Technical and Usage Data

Even if you do not submit personal information, we collect non-personally identifiable information ("Non-Personal Information") about how you interact with our site. This helps us optimize performance, refine the user experience, and ensure high-availability service delivery.

Non-Personal Information may include:

  • IP (Internet Protocol) address and geographic region
  • Browser type, version, and operating system
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP)
  • Referring/exit pages, browsing duration, and interaction paths across property portfolios

3. Cookies, Google Ads & Third-Party Analytics

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance site functionality and analyze user traffic. A cookie is a small text file stored on your device that includes an anonymous unique identifier.

Google Ads & Analytics Disclosure

We utilize third-party analytics and advertising services, including Google Analytics and Google Ads (Remarketing and Conversion Tracking):

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve relevant advertisements based on past visits to our website.
  • These cookies assist in measuring advertising effectiveness, evaluating traffic trends, and presenting pertinent property updates to interested principals.
  • Opt-Out: You can customize your preferences or opt out of Google's personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings. You may also manage cookie preferences directly through your web browser configuration.
  • Withdraw marketing consent at any time by emailing visionexcellenceproperties@outlook.com or requesting cessation via direct communication.

4. How We Use and Share Your Information

We use your personal information solely to provide real estate advisory and brokerage services, process your property inquiries, and communicate relevant market opportunities.

Strict Data Sharing Policy

  • No Selling or Renting Data: We do not sell, trade, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties, commercial brokers, or marketing agencies for their independent commercial purposes.
  • Authorized Developers & Partners: Your personal details are only shared with licensed property developers or essential transaction partners directly involved in fulfilling your specific property acquisition, viewing, or conveyance.
  • Service Providers: We may share data with trusted third-party service providers (such as hosting infrastructure, CRM databases, and technical systems) who assist in running our business operations. These providers are bound by strict confidentiality obligations and are prohibited from utilizing your data for any other purpose.
  • Legal Disclosures: We may disclose personal information if required by UAE law, judicial order, regulatory authorities (including the Real Estate Regulatory Agency — RERA, and the Dubai Land Department), or to protect the safety and rights of our clients and the public.

5. Accessing and Updating Your Data

Your personal information is accessible only to authorized personnel of Vision Excellence Properties who require it to serve your advisory requests. Any client or user who has provided personal data maintains the right to inspect, correct, update, or request the deletion of their personal information from our active records. To request amendments or data removal, please contact us at visionexcellenceproperties@outlook.com.

6. Data Retention

We retain client enquiry and transaction records for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purpose for which they were collected, manage our fiduciary relationship with you, satisfy legal and regulatory mandates, resolve disputes, and enforce our client agreements. When personal data is no longer required, we securely delete or anonymize it in accordance with applicable UAE regulatory retention standards.

7. External Links Disclaimer

This website may contain links to external third-party websites (such as master developer portals, architectural resources, or government registries) for reference and convenience. When navigating to an external link, you leave the Vision Excellence Properties website, and our Privacy Policy no longer applies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, policies, or content of external third-party domains.

8. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard technical, organizational, and cryptographic security protocols to safeguard your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, loss, misuse, or alteration. While we maintain rigorous data protection standards, transmission over the public internet cannot be guaranteed as 100% impenetrable, and users submit communications acknowledging standard internet transmission risks.
